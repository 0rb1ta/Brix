One "Scene widgets" list instead of "Overlays" and "Browser widgets". You pick a
type when adding: donation alert, web page, image or text. Your widgets are
migrated automatically, including which scenes they belong to.

Image and text widgets do not start a browser at all: one WebView cost 9-10% of a
CPU core. Text supports {time}, {battery} and {bitrate}.

Donation alerts are one block now: picture, name with amount, message - and the
whole block scales with its window. The caption is rendered at the frame's own
resolution, so it no longer blurs on a large window.

Fixed: the placement frame lied about the size by 2.75x, so a window dragged to
"half the screen" was stored as 16% of the width. Fixed: white flash at startup.

A snapshot button saves the current frame to the gallery. The HUD shows the
carrier name instead of CELLULAR. An intro animation on launch - drawn by code,
so it adds nothing to the download size.
