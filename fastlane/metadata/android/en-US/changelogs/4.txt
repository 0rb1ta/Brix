Security: the stream key no longer reaches the system log, which is included in the
diagnostics export. If you have shared a log or an export, rotate your key.

The default Moblink password is no longer 1234 — a random one is generated. If you
had a relay paired, enter the new password in Settings → Moblink.

Channel weight is now a rank of 1-10 instead of 0-100: the old scale fed the bonding
algorithm numbers of the wrong magnitude. Your values are converted automatically.

Pick the exact microphone by name, not just the kind of device.

Fixed: numbers no longer freeze after Stop; the "add a server" screen no longer
flashes at launch.
